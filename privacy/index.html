This Privacy Policy describes how MWAH ("MWAH," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit gimmeemwah.com (the "Site"). The Site is intended only for adults 21 years of age or older in jurisdictions where adult-use cannabis is lawful. By using the Site you confirm you meet these requirements and consent to this Policy.

1

Information We Collect

Information you provide

If you submit your email address through our newsletter signup form, we collect that email address. We may associate an approximate state with the signup, derived from the request's IP address, so we can understand where subscriber interest is coming from. This is not GPS or precise location data and may be inaccurate or unavailable. If you contact us, we collect any information you include in your message.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we automatically collect limited technical information, including your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring website, pages viewed, and approximate geographic location (derived from IP address, typically accurate to the city level). This information is used to prevent abuse, secure the Site, and understand aggregate traffic patterns.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies and similar storage mechanisms. A session cookie is set when you confirm you are 21 years of age or older through our age gate — this cookie expires when you close your browser. We use Google Analytics 4 to measure aggregate Site usage, and Mixpanel to understand anonymous Site journeys. Our Mixpanel integration uses a random session-only identifier that expires when the browser tab closes. It does not create a named profile or send names, email addresses, or IP-derived location to Mixpanel.

2

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • ✓ Provide and operate the Site, including the Find Us directory of licensed retailers
  • ✓ Send marketing communications if you have signed up for our newsletter (you may unsubscribe at any time)
  • ✓ Understand the state-level geographic distribution of subscriber interest
  • ✓ Protect the Site from automated abuse, scraping, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • ✓ Measure aggregate traffic and improve the Site
  • ✓ Comply with applicable cannabis advertising, privacy, and consumer-protection laws

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information only in the following circumstances:

Service providers. We share limited information with third-party service providers that help us operate the Site — such as Google (analytics), Mixpanel (anonymous journey analytics), email-delivery platforms, hosting providers, and Cloudflare. These providers process information on our behalf under contractual terms that limit how they can use it.

Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Business transfers. If MWAH is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information we hold may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

4

How Long We Keep Information

Email addresses submitted through our newsletter are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. We retain technical data only as long as reasonably necessary for security and abuse prevention. New signup records are configured so their stored IP address, user agent, and referring URL are deleted after 90 days. Google Analytics and Mixpanel data are retained for no longer than 14 months. The age-gate session cookie and anonymous Mixpanel session identifier expire when you close your browser or tab.

5

Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You submit information at your own risk.

6

Your Choices and Rights

Unsubscribe. Every marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link. Clicking it removes your email from our marketing list immediately.

Access, correction, deletion. You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request deletion. Email contact@gimmeemwah.com to submit a request. We will respond within 45 days.

Do Not Track. Because there is no common industry standard for interpreting Do Not Track signals, we currently do not respond to them. You can still limit tracking using the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, browser privacy features, or content blockers.

7

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) provides you additional rights:

  • Right to know — Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected.
  • Right to delete — Request that we delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to correct — Request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to opt out — We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to non-discrimination — We will not deny service or charge different prices because you exercised any of these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@gimmeemwah.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.
