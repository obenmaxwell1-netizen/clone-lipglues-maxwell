About MWAH

Pretty was always the point. A cannabis brand designed like the rest of her life.

"You see the shape first. Then the color. Then you pick it up and it feels like it was already supposed to be there."

MWAH is a design-forward cannabis brand created with women in mind. Our lip-gloss-shaped 1g all-in-one vape pens are available through licensed cannabis retailers.

From the branding and the box to the lip-gloss silhouette, every detail was designed to look intentional. Not something you hide at the bottom of your bag. Something that belongs with the outfit.

The things you carry become part of your personality — your bag, your jewelry, your lip combo, your phone case. Your pen can have taste too.

Why We Made Her

Cannabis culture needed a new point of view.

Women have always been part of cannabis culture. The products just rarely felt designed with us in mind.

For years, the category was dominated by masculine hardware and purist rules. Anything aesthetic, feminine, or fun could be dismissed as less serious — as if caring about color, packaging, or how something fits into your life made the experience matter less.

We never agreed.
