"You see the shape first. Then the color. Then you pick it up and it feels like it was already supposed to be there."

MWAH brings together distinctive visual design, expressive packaging, and a collection of flavor-inspired cannabis products. Every detail of the presentation is created to give the brand a recognizable identity.

From Strawberry Matcha and Watermelon Wifey to Mango Mamacita and Glow Up Grape, the MWAH collection includes eight distinct varieties. Each product has its own name, visual personality, and product information.

Explore the collection to learn more about each variety, or use our retailer finder to locate licensed cannabis dispensaries carrying MWAH products.
