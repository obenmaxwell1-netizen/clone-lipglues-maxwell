WHY WE MADE HER

Cannabis culture needed a new point of view.

Women have always been part of cannabis culture. The products just rarely felt designed with us in mind.

For years, the category was dominated by masculine hardware and purist rules. Anything aesthetic, feminine, or fun could be dismissed as less serious. As if caring about color, packaging, or how something fits into your life made the experience matter less.

We never agreed.
