"Even the internet's favorite nickname for a vape pen was male-coded. Penjamin had his moment. Pennifer is the feminine remix."

KISS LABS is the creative studio behind MWAH — a cannabis brand that dared to ask a simple question: why does everything in this industry feel like it was designed for someone else?

The answer became MWAH. Lip-gloss shaped. Intention-first. Built for the woman who already knows what she likes, and expects her cannabis to keep up.

The Philosophy

🎨

Every decision — shape, color, packaging, name — is intentional. Nothing is an afterthought.
👑
For Her, By Design
Not in a "pink it and shrink it" way. In a "this genuinely fits your life" way.
💡
Culture Forward
Cannabis culture is bigger than the industry's old defaults. KISS LABS proves it.

The cannabis category has been dominated by masculine hardware and purist rules for too long. KISS LABS exists to prove that thoughtful, beautiful, feminine design is not a compromise — it's a standard.

The Pennifer Era

Internet culture gave us Penjamin — a masculine-coded nickname for a vape pen that became shorthand for the whole category. It was charming. It was funny. And it was also a little telling.

KISS LABS saw Pennifer in its place: the same vibe, different energy. The pen that matches your aesthetic, your mood, your moment.

Pennifer doesn't hide at the bottom of your bag. She belongs with the outfit.
