"Even the internet's favorite nickname for a vape pen was built for someone else. KISS LABS asked: what if it wasn't?"

KISS LABS is the creative studio that brought MWAH Lip Gloss to life. We started with a question that nobody in cannabis was asking: why does all of this still feel like it was made for someone else?

The answer wasn't another muted product in plain packaging. The answer was MWAH — lip-gloss shaped, flavor-first, and built for the woman who already knows exactly what she wants.

The Philosophy

🎨

Every decision — shape, color, packaging, name — is intentional. Nothing is an afterthought.

For Her, By Design

Culture Forward
Cannabis culture is bigger than the industry's old defaults. KISS LABS proves it.

The cannabis category has been dominated by masculine hardware and purist rules for too long. KISS LABS exists to prove that thoughtful, beautiful, feminine design is not a compromise — it's a standard.

The Pennifer Era

Internet culture gave us Penjamin — a charming, masculine-coded nickname for a vape pen that quietly became shorthand for the whole category. Nobody questioned it because nobody had to.

KISS LABS questioned it. And Pennifer arrived: same vibe, completely different energy. A pen that matches your aesthetic, your moment, and your mood — not the other way around.

Pennifer doesn't hide at the bottom of your bag. She belongs with the outfit.
